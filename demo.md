# Melit — 2-minute demo

Hackathon submission video for **SERV Hackathon Edition 02** (Kite track, Reppo track).

- **Who it's for:** people who don't haggle and don't save. Spoken in the first 20 seconds.
- **Where:** https://melit.vercel.app, fresh browser, empty ledger. Nothing is pre-filled.
- **Voice:** soft male, unhurried (calm, low, documentary). Narration below is the exact read.
- **Honest on camera:** sellers are labelled mocks; the Spend Guard is server code; payments are real pieUSD on KiteAI testnet (chain 2368) with a KiteScan link; identity is the testnet wallet, not Passport-verified. Reppo judges in the background and the verdict is shown when it lands, about a minute later.

## Shot list

### 1. Introduction (0:00–0:19)

On the top of the dashboard: the name, the one-line promise, the agent card.

> Most of us pay the sticker price. Haggling feels awkward, and savings quietly leak away. Meet Melit: an AI shopping agent for everyday people who don't know how to negotiate, or how to save. It bargains for you, buys only good deals, and never breaks your weekly budget.

### 2. The agent and the limit (0:19–0:30)

Agent ID card, then the weekly budget gauge at $0 of $300.

> This is Melit's live dashboard. The agent has its own KiteAI testnet wallet, and a hard spending limit: three hundred dollars over a rolling seven days.

### 3. First purchase (0:30–0:41)

Pick **Wireless Earbuds** ($129, Save more), press **Negotiate and buy**, and open a reasoning trail while the rounds stream.

> Let's ask for wireless earbuds, listed at a hundred and twenty-nine dollars. Every move Melit makes is a SERV Reasoning call that returns strict JSON, with its reasoning shown.

### 4. Guard, payment, vault (0:41–0:59)

The green purchase card, the Spend Guard line, the KiteScan transaction, then the Savings Vault at 85/15.

> The seller counters, and Melit closes below list. The Spend Guard approves the exact amount in server code. The sellers are labelled mocks, but the payment is real pieUSD on KiteAI testnet, with the transaction on KiteScan. Eighty-five percent of the savings is yours.

### 5. It learns (0:59–1:16)

In **Deals & feedback**: thumbs down, note "you paid too much", send. Hold on **What Melit learned**, the seller's stars, and the Reppo badge.

> Now I teach it. Thumbs down: you paid too much. SERV turns that note into a small, capped strategy change, so Melit pushes harder next time. The seller rated Melit too, and Reppo's evaluator panel is judging this purchase in the background.

### 6. Second purchase (1:16–1:29)

**Running Shoes** from the seller who bluffs. The updated strategy goes out with the next SERV call.

> Next, running shoes, from a seller who bluffs that every price is his last. The updated strategy now rides along with every SERV prompt.

### 7. The key moment (1:29–1:39)

Press **Try to overspend**. Hold on the red card.

> Now the key moment. Melit tries to buy something that doesn't fit what's left this week. The Spend Guard blocks it in server code, before any payment is even attempted.

### 8. The verdict (1:39–1:51)

The wait itself is not shown. Cut to the returned Reppo verdict, open "Why Reppo said that", and the learning note.

> About a minute later, Reppo's verdict arrives: a score, a route, and the evaluator models behind it. Melit learns from that as well, and tracks whether it's actually getting better.

### 9. Close (1:51–1:59)

**Is Melit getting better?** — the before/after from this session only.

> Melit only earns when it saves you money. Built on SERV Reasoning, paying on KiteAI, judged by Reppo.
